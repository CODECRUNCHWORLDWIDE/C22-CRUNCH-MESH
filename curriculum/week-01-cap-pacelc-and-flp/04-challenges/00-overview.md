# Week 1 — Challenges

The exercises drill the theory. **The challenge makes you the architect in the review.** You're handed a real system's documentation and asked the question a staff engineer asks: "What does this actually guarantee, and where does the marketing disagree with the manual?"

## Index

1. **[Challenge 1 — Audit a real system's consistency claims](./challenge-01-audit-a-real-system.md)** — pick a production data system, read its real documentation, and produce a written audit that places it on the consistency lattice, assigns its PACELC corner, separates its safety guarantees from its liveness guarantees, and names at least one place where its prose overstates what it delivers. (~90 min)

Challenges are optional for passing the week, but this one is the single best preparation for the **midterm architecture-review essay** (end of Week 12), where you do exactly this at length for an open-source distributed system. Do it now in miniature. The skill — reading a system you didn't build and stating its true guarantees in under an hour — is what separates an engineer who "knows CAP" from one who can defend an architecture decision under cross-examination.
