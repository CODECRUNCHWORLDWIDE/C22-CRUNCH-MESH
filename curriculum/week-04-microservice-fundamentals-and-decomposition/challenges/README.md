# Week 4 — Challenges

The exercises drill the mechanics. **The challenge makes you the staff engineer.** You're handed a real monolith and asked to propose a decomposition and *defend it under cross-examination* — the way it actually happens in a design review, where the reviewer's job is to find the alternative you didn't consider.

## Index

1. **[Challenge 1 — Decompose and defend a 40 kLOC monolith](challenge-01-decompose-and-defend.md)** — read a substantial monolithic e-commerce backend, produce a candidate service topology with a context map, and write the decomposition memo that defends it — including three rejected alternatives, each with a specific reason it lost. (~90 min)

Challenges are optional for passing the week, but this one is the single best preparation for the Phase 1 architecture review in Week 12, where you defend your `cart` system's boundaries to two external reviewers. Do it. The skill — proposing a topology *and* demonstrating you explored the space well enough to reject the obvious-but-wrong alternatives — is exactly what separates an engineer who "knows microservices" from one who can lead the design of a platform. A proposal with no rejected alternatives is a proposal a reviewer doesn't trust.
